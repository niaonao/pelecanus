About the project

An open-ended
creative experiment.

A collection of possible worlds.

Pelecanus is an ongoing exploration of AI creativity, where different language models turn prompts into immersive 3D experiences.

Each work preserves the model’s original output. Together, they make space for looking closely at visual expression, spatial construction, motion, and interaction—without declaring a winner.

The original works remain independent, single-file Three.js experiences. Some load Three.js from a CDN and need an internet connection.

Read the original brief 
使用 Three.js 创建一个精致、可交互的 3D 网页场景：一只鹈鹕正在骑自行车。

鹈鹕、自行车、环境、动画、灯光、摄像机和交互均通过程序实现。鹈鹕需要真实地骑自行车，并具有协调的腿部、脚踏、车轮和身体运动。

不要使用 SVG、2D Canvas、外部 3D 模型或预渲染动画。

最终输出一个可以直接在现代浏览器中运行的完整 HTML 文件。
